Objective#

We will use PWM to control 2 wheels to make a shuffle car out of our breadboard.

Step 7: Adding a potentiometer#

We can use a potentiometer to control the speed of our shuffle car. As a reminder, the duty cycle controls the speed of the servos!

You need to connect the potentiometer to your Pico. The potentiometer has 3 pins: VCC, output, and ground. Connect the pin that’s labeled as 1 to ground, the output pin to an analogue pin on the Pico, and the VCC pin to the 3V3(Out) pin on the Pico.

Example:

Now let’s set up the potentiometer in your code:

from machine import ADC
pot = ADC(26)

pot_value = pot.read_u16()

Now, modify your forward(), backward(), right(), and left() functions to use the value of the potentiometer to adjust the speed of the servos. Hint: pot_value ranges from 0 to 65535. What does pot_value/65535 give you?

Make sure you test your code!

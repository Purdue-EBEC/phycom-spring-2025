2.2.3. Shuffle Car#

Materials#

  • USB cable

  • Breadboard

  • Raspberry Pi Pico

  • FS90R micro servo (x2)

  • Wheels (x2)

  • Battery (WIP)

  • Kit Wires

Objective#

We will use PWM to control 2 wheels to make a shuffle car out of our breadboard.

Step 1: Circuit and Breadboard Setup#

Review the documentation of the FS90R here and the Pico here.

Using what we did in the previous lab as reference, connect 2 servos to the Pico. Then, attach a wheel to each of the servos. You are ready to code!
